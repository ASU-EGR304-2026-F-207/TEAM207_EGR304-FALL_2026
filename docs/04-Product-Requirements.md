## Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to reach out to all students but mainly to those living the dorm life and improve students stress from academic deadlines and uncomfortable room climates. We aim to research the aspects of the Arizona State University dorm life to identify any areas of need or improvement. To accomplish our goal of improving student dorm life we plan to design a dorm hub with adaptive technology to update students of their upcoming assignments, the humidity or air condition based on the temperature of the dorm, and adaptive lighting to assist with creating healthy sleep cycles or wake up routines. hello


## Stakeholders

* **Target group** Arizona State University students living in dorms or similar housing, looking for a way to integrate technology into their dorm life to aid them in their academic journey. 
* **Target purchaser** Students or those seeking a gift for students. People that have busy schedules and are intersted in comfort and data integration. 
* **Customer service** Modular design for easy return or replacement of processing unit, online support pages, e-forms to process returns and warranty repairs. 
* **Marketing & Sales division** Multifunction devices with integrated data and controls are becoming more poular. Self care is a daily routine requirement. Saving time and getting better rest is something everyone wants.
* **Retailers** Small form factor, wide temperature tolorance, impact resistant. 

## Use Cases

### User Story #1: Ayn Rand

Ayn enjoyed the independence of living in a campus dorm, but keeping up with college life proved more challenging than she expected. Every week brought a new mix of assignments, shifting class schedules, meetings with advisors, and personal appointments. Relying on sticky notes and memory wasn't enough, and small oversights gradually became a source of unnecessary stress. By the end of many days, she felt mentally drained and often sacrificed sleep to catch up on responsibilities she hadn't planned for.

Rather than letting the chaos continue, Ayn decided to rethink how she managed her time. She adopted a single system to organize her coursework and commitments, scheduled reminders before important deadlines, and built a nightly routine that helped her wind down before bed. The changes didn't eliminate her busy schedule, but they made it far more manageable. With better organization and more restful nights, she felt in control of her days and had more energy to focus on learning and enjoying campus life.

### User Story #2: Aldous Huxley

As Aldous Huxley grew older, he became even more convinced that modern technology was unnecessary. He valued familiar routines and preferred to rely on his own habits rather than digital tools. At the same time, age brought new challenges. Restful nights became harder to come by, and poor sleep began to affect his energy, concentration, and overall health. Although friends suggested that technology might help, Aldous dismissed the idea, believing it would only add complexity to his life.

Eventually, after one too many exhausting mornings, Aldous decided to give a thoughtfully designed digital assistant a chance. To his surprise, it blended naturally into his routine instead of disrupting it. It encouraged healthier sleep habits, helped him maintain a more consistent daily rhythm, and quietly supported his well-being without demanding constant attention. As his sleep improved, so did his energy and outlook. Aldous came to realize that the best technology wasn't about replacing independence—it was about providing just enough support to help him continue living life on his own terms.

## Aspects

The new product design will be based on that of the Sunrise Alarm Clock. The **P1 - P10** is the "code" to indicate the priority of the requirement, from low to high.

1. **Producer Design**
      * 1.0 The product shall be budget friendly. (P6)
      * 1.1 The product shall be visually easy to distinguish from existing Sunrise Alarm Clock. (P2)
      * 1.2 The product shall be easy to recognize as a display device. (P1)
      * 1.3 The product shall avoid styling elements from the common alarm clock. (P3)
      * 1.4 The product shall be made of durable materials. (P10)
      * 1.5 The product shall be reliable. (P9)
      * 1.6 The product shall be easy to set up and use. (P8)
      * 1.7 The product shall minimize table space. (P7)
      * 1.8 The product shall provide enough light for indoor use. (P4)
      * 1.9 The product shall be multifunctional. (P5)
    
2. **Functionality**
      * 2.0 The product shall have a physical switch to turn off wireless communications. (P9)
      * 2.1 The product shall provide multiple types of lighting. (P2)
      * 2.2 The product shall have dual power sources, battery and wall plug. (P3)
      * 2.3 The product shall have at least three output signals. (P1)
      * 2.4 The product shall have at least three input signals. (P2)
      * 2.4 The product shall keep accurate time. (P4)
      * 2.5 The product shall be impact rated. (P10)
      * 2.6 The product shall be able operate 24/7/365 with no loss of power or data. (P3)
      * 2.7 The product shall have intuitive user interface. (P5)
      * 2.8 The product shall occupy less than 1.5 square ft area at rest. (P7)
      * 2.9 The product shall charge other devices via USB-C output. (P10)

3. **Interactivity & User Experience**
      * 3.0 The product shall have buttons and visual feedback. (P10)
      * 3.1 The product shall provide visual and/or audio feedback after 0.5s ov user input. (P10)
      * 3.2 The product shall provide clear status indicators (e.g., charging, Bluetooth connection, alarm enabled, power) that users can understand without referring to the manual. (P5)
      * 3.3 The product shall feature a display that remains readable in both daytime and nighttime conditions through automatic or manual brightness adjustment. (P9)

4. **Customization**
      * 4.0 The product shall allow users to customize light output levels to match different activities (sleep, study, awake, health and relaxation). (P10)
      * 4.1 The product shall allow users need to choose the alarm sound and volume that best suits their preferences.(P7)
      * 4.2 The product shall allow users need to customize the arrangement of multifunction features based on their daily routines. (P8)
      * 4.3 The product shall allow users need to personalize notification sounds and indicator lights to reduce distractions.(P7)

5. **Manufacturing**
      * 5.0 The product shall be designed with fewer than 25 individual parts to simplify assembly and reduce manufacturing costs.(P4)
      * 5.1 The product shall be assembled using standard fasteners or snap-fit features to minimize assembly time.(P4)
      * 5.2 The design shall allow defective electronic components to be replaced without scrapping the entire product during manufacturing.(P10)

6. **Safety
      * 6.0 The product shall operate on a low-voltage power supply (≤24 V DC) to minimize the risk of electric shock.(P1)
      * 6.1 The product shall meet applicable consumer electrical safety standards (e.g., UL, ETL, or CE, depending on the target market). (P10)
      * 6.2 The product shall use insulated wiring and enclosed electrical components to prevent accidental user contact with live circuits.(P10)
